Alexander Trubowitz

Postdoctoral Prize Research Fellow in Politics
Nuffield College, University of Oxford

I am Alexander (Sander) Trubowitz, a Postdoctoral Prize Research Fellow in Politics at Nuffield College, University of Oxford. I work on comparative and international political economy, with a particular focus on the politics of technological change.

In my book manuscript, I develop and test a new theory to explain why compensation and retraining often fail to address the losses workers face from technological displacement, drawing on new archival evidence from Britain and Germany.

My other work is published or forthcoming in The Journal of Politics, Comparative Political Studies, Political Behavior, and Socio-Economic Review.

I completed my Ph.D. in Political Science at Yale University in 2025 and graduated summa cum laude from Tufts University in Classics and Political Science. I have been a visiting researcher at the University of Konstanz and the University of Bonn.
